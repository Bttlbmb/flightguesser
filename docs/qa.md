## Browser regression checklist

Use actual game files with the recorded collection or labeled local fixtures. Keep provider requests blocked for layout/rule checks. Review phone, tablet and desktop sizes, both sides of the 700 px breakpoint, phone landscape and a short viewport to simulate the space available above a keyboard. Include enlarged text and long unbroken labels. Check the focused input as well as its popup. Confirm screenshot capture does not change device emulation or the book's state.

1. **Start and recovery.** Open recorded play directly, cancel during each local-data load, retry a failed load and return home. Failed or cancelled openings must not skip an unseen recording or reopen later. New view headings and mode labels must be visible. Normal/Hard selection persists across entry and recovery views, applies only to the next round, and stays fixed while playing. The finite collection and replay boundary should be clear.
2. **City selection.** Search by city, airport name and code, including accents and long labels. Keep same-name cities distinguishable. Test ArrowDown, ArrowUp, Enter, Escape, clear and Tab. The popup stays outside the Tab sequence; the active option has a visible marker and valid accessible state. Result-count wording must describe the shown list honestly.
3. **One round.** Select a city, reveal an extra clue, submit a miss, type again and submit a duplicate. Revealing the clue uses one guess, fills the next board line and reduces the remaining count. Selection survives a reveal and the phone book toggle; duplicate/invalid input consumes no attempt. Every new clue appears once in the book, separately from the answer rows. On phone, Hide/Show leaves the clue count and globe visible without spending a guess. Rotation must retain the control and collapsed choice. A new clue reopens the book; an invalid, duplicate or exhausted-clue guess preserves its state. Resizing to desktop must show all clues, and returning to phone must restore the player's choice. The new clue and next guess action remain reachable. Check keyboard focus and coarse-pointer behavior separately; after all clues are given, a miss focuses the latest row on a coarse pointer without claiming a new clue.
4. **Scoring and results.** Seoul and Incheon both win ICN; only Seoul wins GMP. Tokyo wins HND and NRT; Narita wins only NRT. Distinct Portlands stay separate. A win or six used attempts, including paid clues, ends submissions. Check both all-city and mixed clue/city rounds, including early wins. A linked-city win leads with the successful city and omits distance/direction; every result retains the exact airport, accepted alternatives, source and uncertainty. Guess and clue history stays visible, with remaining facts openly shown and no duplicate clue entries.
5. **Clues and geography.** Direction is labeled **First clue** inside the book; the airplane graphic remains outside it. All revealed clue contents can be read together by opening the phone book, and stay open on desktop. The first missed city creates one combined distance/direction clue that stays fixed; later guesses never add or update city-distance or direction readings. Paid reveals before the first city keep their original clue indexes. Country and remaining distance precede the optional airline, with the accepted city initial last. An airline name needs an exact reported-code match and is skipped if missing. Telemetry and origin never appear as clues. Missing facts are skipped; the final initial clue includes all accepted cities. Exhausting the clues does not end the six-attempt round. Normal mode retains kilometre values; Hard mode uses ranges for both destination distances, including near-zero values and band boundaries. The answer and aircraft stay fixed. No route or unrevealed clue appears before the end. Check a date-line route, polar direction and far-side destination with labeled fixtures, retaining complete text when geometry is clipped.
6. **Accessibility and errors.** Check visible focus, search/clue/result announcements, dialog closure, reduced motion, forced colors and long text. Review loading, unavailable local files, malformed data, denied/timed-out location and a controlled live failure. Distinguish browser/network failures from a proven CORS diagnosis.
7. **Optional browser tools.** Search and submission use the same city IDs and guards as the UI. Read/search state never exposes a hidden answer or device coordinates. The combined first-guess clue is visible only after that miss; later guess records carry no extra distance/direction readings. A correct first guess creates no reference-city clue. Invalid tool input, duplicates, finished rounds and exhausted clues leave state unchanged.
